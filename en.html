Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Project owner and privacy contact: ACAP1.0@OUTLOOK.COM · United States

This policy summarizes how the current version of Nap-less works. It is general information and does not constitute legal advice.

Device biometrics

Nap-less uses your operating system's biometric authentication (fingerprint or face) to confirm it is you before opening the challenge that turns off the alarm. The check is performed by Android or iOS: Nap-less only receives a “success” or “failure” result.

Nap-less does not access your fingerprints or your face, does not create or store biometric templates, and does not send them to any server. To change the registered biometrics, use your device settings.

Camera and challenge photos

To prove you completed the challenge you chose, Nap-less asks you to take a photo with the camera. The photo is analyzed on your device, using ML Kit on Android and Vision on iOS, to look for visual cues of the challenge (for example, a glass or a posture). The photo is not sent to our servers, is not saved to your gallery, and is deleted from the app's temporary storage after analysis.

The result is a visual cue only: it does not certify that you performed an action or took a medication.

Permissions the app requests

  • Camera: to take the challenge photo.
  • Biometrics / Face ID: to verify your identity through the system.
  • Notifications and exact alarms: so the alarm rings at the chosen time, including after a phone restart.
  • Device audio: only if you choose your own sound file as a ringtone.
  • Background, vibration and screen wake: to keep the alarm ringing.

Storage and accounts

You do not need to create an account. Your alarms and preferences (language, theme and text size) are stored locally on your device, and security settings are stored using the system's secure storage. Nap-less does not upload photos or biometric data to servers.

Ads and tracking

Nap-less does not include advertising, advertising tracking or analytics tools inside the app.

How to delete your data

You can remove the biometrics setup from the app. To also delete your local alarms and settings, clear Nap-less data from your device or uninstall the app.

Hosting of this page

This public page is served through GitHub Pages. The host may process technical information about requests under its own practices; Nap-less does not receive photos or biometric data as a result.

For privacy questions, write to ACAP1.0@OUTLOOK.COM.

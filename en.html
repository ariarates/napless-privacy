Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Project owner and privacy contact: ACAP1.0@OUTLOOK.COM · United States

This policy explains what data Nap-less handles, where it is stored and which third-party services are involved. It is general information and does not constitute legal advice.

Overview

Nap-less has no servers of its own. Your account, alarms, sleep diary, recordings and pulse measurements stay on your device. The only data that leaves the phone is the crash reports sent to Firebase Crashlytics, update checks with Expo and the PayPal site if you choose to donate.

Local account

To use the app you create an account that is stored only on your device: email address, an internal identifier and a cryptographic hash of your password. No email is sent and no server account is created. The verification code is generated and checked on the phone itself.

Your email address is used only to identify the account on the device. It is not used for marketing and is not shared with third parties.

Device biometrics

Nap-less uses your operating system's biometric authentication (fingerprint or face) to confirm it is you before opening the challenge that turns off the alarm. The check is performed by Android or iOS: Nap-less only receives a “success” or “failure” result.

Nap-less does not access your fingerprints or your face, does not create or store biometric templates, and does not send them to any server. To change the registered biometrics, use your device settings.

Camera: challenge photo and pulse measurement

Challenge photo. To prove you completed the challenge you chose, Nap-less asks you to take a photo with the camera. The photo is analyzed on your device, using ML Kit on Android and Vision on iOS, to look for visual cues of the challenge (for example, a cup of water, the pages of a book or a posture). The photo is not sent to our servers, is not saved to your gallery, and is deleted from the app's temporary storage after analysis.

Pulse measurement (PPG). If you start a measurement, the app uses the rear camera and the flashlight for a few seconds to estimate your heart rate and heart rate variability. Frames are processed on the device and discarded; only the numeric result is stored in the encrypted database described below.

The challenge result is a visual cue only: it does not certify that you performed an action or took a medication.

Microphone and sleep recordings

Sleep tracking is an optional feature you start manually. While it is active, the app keeps the microphone listening through a foreground service with a permanent notification, and it only keeps audio clips that exceed certain sound thresholds (for example, snoring, speech or coughing).

Each clip is encrypted with AES-GCM using a key stored in the system keystore, and is deleted automatically after 24 hours. You can delete any individual recording from inside the app at any time.

Keep in mind that the microphone may also capture other people in the room. Enable this feature only if everyone present is aware of it.

Health data and sleep diary

Nap-less stores in an encrypted database (SQLCipher, with the key in the system's secure storage) your sleep sessions, detected events (snoring, speech, coughing, with time, duration and confidence), pulse and variability measurements, and diary entries: caffeine, alcohol, exercise minutes and mood.

This data is used only to show you summaries and correlations inside the app. It is not sent to any server, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising. Nap-less is not a medical product and this information is not intended to diagnose or treat any condition.

Permissions the app requests

When you create your account, Nap-less requests camera and notifications, the permissions needed for challenges and alarms. The rest are requested only when you use the related feature. You can deny any permission and keep using the rest of the app; the related feature stays unavailable until you allow it in your system settings.

  • Camera: for the challenge photo and the pulse measurement.
  • Microphone: only if you enable sleep tracking.
  • Notifications and exact alarms: so the alarm rings at the chosen time, including after a phone restart.
  • Biometrics / Face ID: to verify your identity through the system.
  • Audio file access: only if you choose your own sound as a ringtone. Nap-less does not access your photos or videos.
  • Foreground service, vibration and screen wake: to keep the alarm and sleep tracking running.
  • Ignoring battery optimizations: so the system does not stop the alarm or the recording.
  • Internet: for crash reports, update checks and the donation link.

Third-party services

Nap-less does not sell or share data with advertisers. These services may process technical data under their own policies:

  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google): receives reports when the app crashes, together with the device model and OS version, the app version and an installation identifier. It does not include your recordings, diary or measurements.
  • Expo / EAS Update: when checking for updates, it receives technical data such as your IP address, platform, app version and update channel.
  • PayPal: if you tap the donation button, its website opens in your browser. Nap-less does not send it any of your data, but PayPal will process your IP address and payment details under its own policy.

Ads and tracking

Nap-less does not include advertising or behavioural analytics, does not build advertising profiles and does not track your activity in other apps or websites. The only diagnostic component is the crash reporting service described above.

Data retention

Audio recordings are deleted automatically after 24 hours. All other local data (account, alarms, settings, diary, events and measurements) stays on the device until you delete it from the app or uninstall Nap-less.

How to delete your data

You can delete individual recordings from the sleep screen and remove the biometrics setup from the app.

To delete your account and all local data, use Delete account and data on your account screen. The account, alarms, encrypted recordings, sleep database and settings are permanently deleted. You can also do it by clearing Nap-less data from your device settings or uninstalling the app.

If you need help deleting your data, write to ACAP1.0@OUTLOOK.COM.

Your rights

Because data is stored on your device, you can exercise access, rectification, erasure and portability directly from the app or by deleting its data. If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California, you can also contact us with any privacy question or complaint.

Nap-less does not sell or share personal data for advertising purposes.

Children

Nap-less is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child's data has been stored, contact us so we can delete it.

Changes to this policy

If the app's features or the services it uses change, we will update this page and the last-updated date. Significant changes will be announced inside the app.

Hosting of this page

This public page is served through GitHub Pages. The host may process technical information about requests under its own practices; Nap-less does not receive photos or biometric data as a result.

For privacy questions, write to ACAP1.0@OUTLOOK.COM.
